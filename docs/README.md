# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory contains OctoAcme’s lightweight, repeatable guidance for delivering cross-functional projects. The process is customer-first and iterative, with clear ownership, evidence-informed decisions, and space for feedback and learning.

## Project lifecycle at a glance

1. **Initiate** — Validate the business need and measurable outcomes, identify stakeholders, outline the timeline and risks, and decide whether to proceed to planning.
2. **Plan** — Turn the approved initiative into an actionable backlog. Agree on scope, acceptance criteria, estimates, Definition of Done, milestones, resources, risks, and dependencies.
3. **Execute and track** — Deliver in small increments with named ownership. Track work on the project board, use reviews and testing to maintain quality, monitor progress and risks, and escalate blockers through the agreed path.
4. **Release** — Confirm acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and rollback or mitigation readiness. Verify the deployment and communicate the outcome.
5. **Close and improve** — Hold a retrospective after a sprint, release, milestone, or incident. Record a small set of actionable improvements with owners and due dates, then follow up on them.

Communication cadence should fit the project: teams use standups and regular delivery or PM/Product syncs, provide stakeholder updates (often weekly or monthly), and demo at the end of a sprint or milestone. Keep status in a shared source of truth. Core artifacts include the project one-pager or charter, roadmap and release plan, backlog, Definition of Done, risk register, status updates, and retrospective actions.
